How it works

Four steps. All in Google Docs.

01Write your notesAfter a lesson, add notes, new words and homework to your daily Doc.
02Mark it readySet the row to Prepare email. That is the only button.
03Read the draftIt appears in the same Doc. Change anything you like.
04It is sentThe lesson is added to that student's own Doc, so you keep a clean record.

Lives where you already work.

Nothing to install and no new place to check. You always see the e-mail before it goes out, and everything stays in your own Google Drive.

Your Google account

It asks for three things. Nothing more.

SendSend e-mail as youSo the follow-up comes from your own address. It cannot read, search or delete your mail.
FilesOnly the files it createsOne folder in your Drive with your daily Docs, a Doc per student and your student list. It cannot see anything else you keep there.
YouYour e-mail addressSo it knows which account connected. Nothing else from your profile.

You can disconnect at any time in your Google Account, under Security. Details are in the privacy policy and the terms.
