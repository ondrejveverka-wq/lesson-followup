Culture and Corrida

Customs that look strange from the outside: bullfighting in Spain, Black Friday in the USA, the Czech Easter whip. What makes a tradition yours, and can it be part of who you are and still be wrong?

  • Vocabularyshocking, cruel, to be used to, custom, ritual, national identity, ban, outsider
