Who and what

Lesson Follow-up is provided by Ondřej Veverka, an English teacher in the Czech Republic (onrejveverka@gmail.com). It writes follow-up e-mails from your lesson notes, sends them from your Gmail after you have had time to read them, and keeps a Google Doc of lessons for each student. By connecting your Google account you agree to these terms.

Your account and your content

You connect your own Google account and you can disconnect it at any time. Your notes, your student list, the Docs and the e-mails are yours. You give the tool permission to use them only to provide the service to you, as described in the privacy policy.

What you are responsible for

  • Your students' details. You enter only students you teach, with addresses you are allowed to write to, and you tell them you use the tool.
